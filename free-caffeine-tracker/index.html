Free Caffeine Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Data stored on your watch

Free Caffeine Tracker is developed by Bruun Software. The app stores logged drinks, caffeine amounts, timestamps, favorite drinks, settings and summarized consumption history locally on your watch. A small local snapshot supports glances and watch-face readings.

The 256 newest drinks by consumption time retain their details. One additional last-logged drink may be retained for Undo. Older intake is summarized.

The app does not transmit this information to Bruun Software. Bruun Software has no server copy of your drink history.

Offline operation and access

Logging and caffeine estimates work offline. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, internet access, phone communications, location access, sensor access or health-profile access.

Background updates and watch faces

On supported watches, a background service refreshes local estimates. Current caffeine, today’s intake and the bedtime estimate are available to compatible watch faces through Garmin’s complication system. Other watch faces have their own privacy practices.

Managing your data

You can edit or delete recent drinks, undo the last drink and clear history in Settings. You can also change favorites and settings on the watch.

Garmin services and website hosting

Garmin’s Store and platform services are separate from the app and are governed by Garmin’s privacy policies.

This privacy-policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub operates the hosting service and may process information about website visits under its own privacy practices.

Privacy and support contact

Information you voluntarily send by support email is separate from the app’s local data.

For privacy or support questions, email bruunsoftware@gmail.com.
